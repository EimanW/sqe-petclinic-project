## Choices allowed by the brief

- Section 1 gives `tests/team/` as an example folder. The requirement is separation of original tests from upstream. Section registration remains mandatory.
- Section 3 permits the two usability tests to count toward use-case automation if they satisfy the flow assertions. The improvement can be in the MIS or on a team-owned HTML page. Automated checks meet the brief without participants or manual walkthroughs.
- Section 4 gives examples of controls. Choose controls relevant to Basic authentication and the actual browser architecture. OWASP Dependency-Check is appropriate for this Java project. The project contract also asks about Angular dependency coverage where practical.
- Section 5 gives example limits and a possible test-double approach. Fix actual thresholds in advance and demonstrate persistence through the controlled failure and retry.
- The checkpoint cadence, numeric GQM formulas to be chosen with the team, separate accessibility evidence format details, and preference for `tests/team/e2e/` come from the project contract or later team decisions. Cite the brief for its mandatory outcome and the contract for implementation choices.
